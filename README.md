# Solstice Reborn
_original mod by WulfMarius_
_updated by Starbuck097_

An updated version of the old WulfMarius mod for The Long Dark, which adds a change of seasons to the game. The daylight hours gradually lengthen from the winter solstice to the summer solstice and shorten again; along with the length of the day, the illumination and temperature change.

The mod no longer requires a custom mode: it can be enabled in a regular game and in an already started save. The settings are located in the Mod Settings menu and are saved between launches.

**Feautures:**

- The length of the day. The light day is the shortest in winter, the longest in summer. The transition is smooth, without sudden jumps.

- Illumination. During the day, the light is brighter in summer, dimmer in winter.

- Temperature. The weather is colder in winter and a little warmer in summer.

- Seasonal cycle. The full cycle can last 4, 6, or 12 gaming months.

- The duration of the game day itself remains the same — only the time of sunrise and sunset change.

**Updates from Starbuck097:**

- Mod port from version 1.44 to 2.60 (latest).

- Fine-tune the length of each season (from 0 to 12 months).

- "Winter wildlife die-off" setting. When it is turned on, the number of animals varies depending on the season. In late autumn, the population of all species decreases, there is practically no spawn in winter, and animals return by spring. I thought this was a very good concept, since in many polar or circumpolar regions there are practically no animals to be found in winter, with rare exceptions. The setting is recommended for players who love hardcore survival. 

**Dependencies:**

- ModSettings
